argument-hint: "<task>"

Before any edits: plan $@.

Output, as a numbered list:
1. Goal and scope — what the task should accomplish.
2. Files to create or change — the smallest set, with one line each.
3. Implementation steps — the simplest order of work.
4. Anything that must NOT change — existing behavior, APIs, or content to preserve.
5. Risks, assumptions, or open questions — only items that could affect the plan.

Do not include a testing phase. Stop after the plan and wait for me to say "go" before editing anything.
